# Application Settings (customer app)

`/dashboard/settings`, built like Performance, Maintenance and Billing: a hero (picture with a ring showing how complete the profile is, name, plan, member since, sites, devices), a row of ten coloured tabs (the two-column pages use gap-free CSS columns, so a short card leaves no hole), and the tab's cards at the full width of the page (two columns on a wide screen, one on a phone); the open tab is kept in the address (`#security`). Code: `apps/web/src/components/dashboard/settings/`.

## Units

`lib/unit-prefs.ts` reads the choices and converts a reading for display (`displayValue`), `lib/chart-units.ts` applies them to the trend charts (a different scale and label, totals following the power unit). The dashboard layout holds them in `UnitsProvider`; client components read them with `useUnits()` / `useSeriesUnit()`, and the shared field cards take them as a `units` prop so a server-rendered page can pass `getUnitPrefs()` (`lib/unit-prefs-server.ts`). Covered: Overview cards and flow, Monitoring, Performance, Reports screens, EV cards and charts, the field cards, temperatures. Not converted: marketing pages, PDF/CSV downloads, e-mails, weather, prose descriptions, and a few server-rendered tables (EV session list, cost analytics).

## How the parts work

- **E-mail change.** `requestEmailChange` stores a keyed hash of the code (valid 10 minutes, 5 wrong tries, one resend a minute) and e-mails the code to the new address through Resend; `confirmEmailChange` checks it, then changes the sign-in address through the auth admin API (marked confirmed) and `profiles.email`, and tells the old address. Needs `SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY`, `RESEND_API_KEY`, `RESEND_FROM_EMAIL` on the web app. Without `RESEND_API_KEY` no code is sent and the person is told so.
- **Idle sign-out.** `IdleSignOut` (mounted in the dashboard layout) keeps the last-activity time in `localStorage` (shared by the tabs of one browser) and signs out when it is older than the person's limit, on the timer, when the tab becomes visible, and on arrival (so a browser reopened after a long absence is signed out at once). It is checked in the browser while the app is open: it cannot sign out a browser that stays closed.
- **Signing out never ends other devices' sessions** unless asked (`signOut({ scope: "local" })`); "other devices" and "all devices" use Supabase's `others` and `global` scopes. Ending one other device's session is `waytara.revoke_my_session` (`auth.sessions` is not readable by the app; `my_sessions()` shows only the caller's own rows).
- **Overview background pictures** live in the same public `avatars` bucket (`<person's id>/overview-<ms>.jpg`); the server action refuses any path outside the person's own folder, and deletes the picture a new one replaces.

## Saving

A setting that is a switch, a choice or a button saves itself the moment it changes (`use-auto-save.ts`: shown at once, saved one after another so quick changes cannot arrive out of order, put back with an error if a save fails, a short "Saved." otherwise): the notification switches, the units, the Overview cards (once four are chosen) and background (a picture is uploaded when chosen, the darkening is saved when the handle is let go), staying signed in, appearance, start page and site. A Save button stays only where the customer types: the name and phone, the addresses and billing details, the alert and e-mail dialogs. The two-column pages (`TwoColumns` in `settings-card.tsx`) share their cards between two stacks, so no card overlaps another and a short one leaves no hole.

## The sidebar

Support, Home and Sign out are rows like the pages above them, always visible; the account banner at the foot (picture, name, plan) opens Application Settings. Nothing unfolds.

## A new picture shows at once

The sidebar's account banner and the header are rendered from what the sign-in check read before the save, so after saving a picture or a name the settings announce it (`components/profile-live.tsx`) and the banner, the Settings header and the Profile card show it straight away; the page is also re-read from the server.

## Account e-mails and the switches

`lib/notification-prefs.ts` lists the categories and reads a stored choice (`wants(prefs, key)`). Every sender checks its own key: the alert cron per e-mail (`alertKey`: offline, back online, fault, alarm), the device digest per line (`digestKey`), the tariff cron (`tariff`), the support mailer (`support_emails`), and the account e-mails sent by `lib/auth-notify.ts` after a sign-in (login action), a sign-out (sign-out button, a device ended from Settings, others, all, or idle) and a password change (always sent). They are sent with `after()`, so they never slow the page, and carry the device, the place (Vercel's geo headers, when present), the address and the time.

## My alerts ("tell me when the battery is below 20%")

Settings > Alerts. `lib/alert-rules.ts` has the catalogue: 15 things to watch (battery level, charging and discharging power, battery temperature; solar power and energy today; grid import and export power and energy today; consumption power and energy today; electricity rate, grid cost today, value of the solar energy today) and 13 smart alerts (the ready-made ones). Switched on for everyone from the start: battery below 20% and below 10%, grid import above 5 kW, energy bought today above 15 kWh, grid cost today above ₹150. The rest are off until chosen. A customer can switch any off, change its limit, e-mail/app choices and cooldown, or make alerts of their own (any metric, above or below a limit, one site or all, e-mail and/or the bell, repeat no more often than 15 minutes to a day).

- **Storage:** `alert_rules` (a customer's own alerts, and a row per ready-made alert they changed; RLS: own rows, own sites, at most 60) and `alert_rule_state` (server only: whether a rule is tripped and when it last fired).
- **Evaluation:** `/api/cron/evaluate-alert-rules` every 5 minutes (pg_cron, like the other cron routes). Per customer and site it works out each metric from the latest readings of the site's inverters (readings older than 30 minutes are ignored), then: a limit just crossed and not fired within its cooldown fires once (a row for the bell, and one e-mail listing everything that fired, if the customer keeps "E-mail for my alerts" on); a value back in range by a small margin arms the rule again and marks its bell alert cleared. `?dry=1` reports what would fire and writes nothing.
- Needs `CRON_SECRET`, `SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY`, `RESEND_API_KEY` and `CUSTOMER_APP_URL` on the web app, like the other jobs.

## Account activity, two-factor sign-in and pausing

- **Account activity.** `lib/auth-notify.ts` writes a row to `account_events` (service role only; the customer can only read their own, RLS) for every sign-in, sign-out, idle sign-out, device ended, password change, e-mail change and two-factor change, and prunes rows older than 90 days. The e-mail is separate and follows the notification switch.
- **Two-factor sign-in (TOTP).** Settings > Security enrols an authenticator app (QR and secret), verifies one code, and can turn it off with a code. After the password, `login/actions.ts` redirects to `/login/verify` when the account has a verified factor and the session is not yet at AAL2; `proxy.ts` sends any dashboard request from such a session there too (fail-open if the check errors). `verify-form.tsx` calls `mfa.challengeAndVerify`, then `completeSignIn()` records the sign-in and returns the start page. Turning two-factor on makes Supabase end the person's other sessions. There is no recovery code: a lost phone is handled by support (an admin removes the factor).
- **Pausing e-mail.** `profiles.notification_preferences.paused_until`; `wants()` returns false for everything except `auth_signin`/`auth_signout` while it is in the future, and the account e-mails for password, e-mail and two-factor changes are always sent.

## Staying signed in

`lib/session-policy.ts` and `components/dashboard/idle-sign-out.tsx` (mounted in the dashboard layout). The person chooses: an idle time (presets from 5 minutes to 1 week, or their own from 5 minutes to 30 days) with an optional "still there?" warning (30 s, 1 min, 5 min) and a Stay signed in button; a longest session (8 h to 30 days, or their own up to 90 days) counted from when this browser's session was first seen here (keyed by the sign-in token's session id); signing out when the browser is closed (every tab heartbeats into localStorage; a tab that opens with no heartbeat for 20 s, in a session that was already running, means the browser was closed); and whether idle and time-limit sign-outs cover this device or all devices. Each sign-out records its own account event and e-mail. It is checked in the browser while the app is open: a browser that stays closed is not signed out, and close-the-browser is best effort.

## Changing the password

Needs a code: `requestPasswordChange` e-mails a 6-digit code to the account's own address (10 minutes, 5 tries, one a minute, stored hashed in `password_change_codes`); `confirmPasswordChange` checks it and the new password's strength, sets it through the auth admin API, e-mails the "password changed" notice, records the activity and signs out every session (`signOut({ scope: "global" })`); the page then goes to the sign-in page with a message.

## Invoices

`lib/invoice.ts` (types, the GST split, numbers, CSV; tested), `lib/invoice-data.ts` (builds an invoice for one paid payment of the signed-in customer), `lib/invoice-pdf.tsx` (react-pdf), `/api/account/invoice/<payment id>` (PDF; `?download=1` saves it), and `billing-actions.ts` (`getInvoice` for the in-app view, `emailInvoice` with the PDF attached). Prices include GST (Terms 5.4), so an invoice splits the amount paid into the value before tax and the GST in it at the quotation's rate. The invoice number is `WT-<yyyymm>-<start of the payment id>`: not a legal sequential series. "Tax invoice" and the registered address appear only when `WAYTARA_GSTIN` and `WAYTARA_ADDRESS` (lines separated by `|`) are set on the web app.

## Not done

- Phone numbers are not verified.
- Two-factor sign-in has no recovery codes and no SMS option.
- Account deletion is a request to the team, not self-service (devices and warranty are involved).
- There is no data download any more (it was removed on request); the readings can still be downloaded as reports.
- Invoice numbers are not a legal sequential series, and `WAYTARA_GSTIN` / `WAYTARA_ADDRESS` are not set anywhere yet.
- The admin app has no settings page (its sign-out is this-device-only).
